# December 10 — three ways to 300 Luma signups

The event is Thursday, December 10, 2026, 5:00–8:00 p.m. The number to hit is **300 registrations on Luma** by the end of Tuesday, December 8. Today is Tuesday, November 17, so the working window is three weeks, with a dead stretch in the middle.

Each plan below is written to reach 300 on its own. They pull from the same city, so running two of them means the same person can be asked twice. Count a registration once. Use one Luma question on every plan so the source is visible.

## Pick a plan

| Plan | Where the 300 come from | What it takes | When it fails |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Borrowed lists](01-borrowed-lists.md) | Career centers, Handshake, and student-org inboxes send a link they already know how to send | About 25 emailed asks this week, then a second send December 1–3 | A center agrees and then sends after December 7, when San José State is in finals |
| [Campus ground game](02-campus-ground-game.md) | Class announcements, club meetings, and tabling while students are still on campus | People on campus November 18–24 and December 1–4 | Announcements start after Thanksgiving. There is no second full week of class |
| [Workforce and veterans](03-workforce-and-veterans.md) | Job centers, the county veterans office, the library, and adult education refer people who want a skill | Phone calls this week, flyers in lobbies, one library or job-center email | San José Public Library and both workforce boards all decline. Counselor mentions alone will not reach 300 |

Borrowed lists is the highest-volume bet. The ground game is the one that does not wait on a staffer to hit send. Workforce and veterans is the one that fills the room with career changers and veterans, which the evening is built for and a student blast will under-deliver.

## The calendar that all three have to obey

- **November 17–21.** Asks go out. First sends and first tabling happen. A plan that is still “drafting the email” on Friday is behind.
- **November 25.** San José State has no classes. San José City College has no classes November 26–29. Campuses are closed November 26–27.
- **December 1–4.** Second sends, second tabling. This is the last week students are in class.
- **December 7.** Last day of instruction at San José State. Stop starting new campus asks.
- **December 8.** Study day at San José State. Last day that counts toward 300.
- **December 9–11 and 14–15.** San José State finals. The event is the evening of the second day of finals.
- **December 10.** San José City College’s fall semester ends the same day.

Signups can still arrive from a link sent earlier. New institutional asks after December 4 mostly turn into January conversations.

A registration is not a seat filled. At a usual weeknight show rate, 300 Luma registrations put something like 120–180 people in the room, and finals week will push the show rate down. Confirm the room can hold the people you actually expect before the third week is spent filling a waitlist.

## One Luma page before the first send

The page has to show, in this order: free, December 10, 5:00–8:00 p.m., the San Jose place, and who it is for. Experienced professionals, students and new graduates, and career changers. What each person leaves with is a method already used on their own task, an introduction matched to that work, and a next step. CalMU’s programs are that next step for students, veterans, and career changers. They are not the subject line.

Check the counts every morning. The checkpoints inside each plan are the decision, not the end-of-month total.

## Shared floor

CalMU’s San Jose students, faculty, and local inquiries get a direct send in every plan. That list is the only audience you control on day one. Pull the real count before assigning it a target. The plans use 40 registrations from that list as a placeholder until the export exists.

If a plan is short on the morning of December 1, the panelists each post the Luma link once the same day. That post is a reserve. It is not already counted inside the 300.
