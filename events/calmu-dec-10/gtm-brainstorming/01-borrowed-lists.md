# Plan A — Borrowed lists

Get to 300 by having institutions send the Luma link through lists they already operate: career-center email, Handshake, a student-org blast, a veterans-center note. One forwardable paragraph does most of the work. The signup happens because a counselor or a club president hit send, not because someone tabled.

This is the plan to run if a few yeses from San José State and the larger community colleges are realistic this week. It is a weak plan if those asks sit in an inbox until after Thanksgiving.

## Who it signs up

Mostly students and new graduates, plus the working adults who read a campus career email. San José State is the volume. Santa Clara University, the law school there, and the community-college career centers are the rest. Veterans come from campus veterans offices, not from the VA.

A career-center send will under-represent career changers who are not enrolled. That group is Plan C.

## The email to a career center

Subject: Free student workshop, Dec 10 — one paragraph you can forward

> I’m writing from the Singularity Workshop with California Miramar University. On Thursday, December 10, 5:00–8:00 p.m. in San Jose, we are holding a free evening for students, new graduates, and career changers.
>
> People bring a piece of work they want to show an employer, use one AI method on it, and meet someone already hired into the field they are entering. A short panel opens the exercise. Practitioners from Mozilla, JLINC, and the AI-at-work community are on it. CalMU’s accelerated programs are there as a next step for anyone who wants one. There is no fee and no application.
>
> Would you forward the paragraph below, or list it on Handshake, once this week and once on December 1 or 2? I can send a square image and a one-page flyer. Happy to be listed as a community workshop rather than as a recruiter.
>
> Forwardable paragraph:
>
> Thursday, December 10, 5:00–8:00 p.m. in San Jose. Free. Bring a task from a class, an internship, or a job you want. You will use one AI method on it, meet someone already working in the field you are entering, and leave with a next step you can name. Register: [Luma link]

Swap the middle sentence when writing the veterans center: “Built for veterans using education benefits and for anyone changing careers. You try the skill once and you can name it afterward. Staff can answer questions about education benefits if you want that conversation.”

Swap it for the law school and for working-professional programs: “Bring a task you already own. You take it through a tool and finish the part the tool cannot: judgment, clients, and exceptions. You meet peers who use AI on that kind of work.”

## Week by week

**November 17–18.** Luma page is complete, including place and the “how did you hear” question. Send the CalMU house email the same day. Send every ask in the contact table. The SJSU Career Center and SOPP go first.

**November 19–21.** Call anyone who has not answered: SJSU career desk, De Anza, Mission, SJCC. A phone call on day three beats a second email on day six. Log a send date for each yes. Target for Friday: at least six partners have a send date, and registrations are at 50. If the SJSU Career Center has not answered, call again Friday morning and treat silence as a signal to start Plan B on Monday for that 110.

**November 23–25.** Anything that can still reach a campus inbox goes out Monday or Tuesday. November 25 is a non-instructional day at San José State. Do not schedule a first send for the week of Thanksgiving and expect students to see it before the break.

**November 26–29.** Leave the partners alone. Watch Luma. A link sent on November 24 will keep registering people through the weekend. Answer registration questions the same day. Silence over a holiday reads as a dead event.

**December 1–3.** Second send to every partner who sent once. The note is one line: “Same free workshop, Thursday evening, December 10. Seats are open: [link].” Personal follow-up to the two or three partners whose first send produced almost nothing. Ask them to put it in a different channel, a club Slack or an instructor note, rather than to send the same email again.

**December 4–8.** No new institutions. Fill the gap from the channels that already converted. Close at 300 on December 8. After that, write to registrants about what to bring: a task, a piece of work they want to show, or a conversation they want to think through.

## Where the registrations leak

Career centers protect student inboxes, and a December event from an unknown sender looks like recruiting. The email above asks for a community listing and says there is no fee. If a center asks for a Handshake employer account, open one. If they ask for a two-week lead, say the date is December 10 and a send on December 1 still helps. If they can only offer a January slot, thank them and drop them from this event.

Handshake listings convert when the title is a workshop a student would attend, and they stall when the title is a university information session. Use “Free AI workshop: use it on your own work, December 10.”

Club presidents will forward a short paragraph from a phone. They will not forward an attachment and a paragraph about accreditation.
