This evening is for:
- **Experienced Professionals** who want to keep their jobs by knowing how to apply AI to their marketing and business development workflows.
- **Students and New Graduates** who want to have the right AI job skills that move them to the front of the line when applying for jobs.
- **Career Changers** who understand AI changes the entire job landscape, and want to move into a more lucrative AI Native career path.

Each month we focus on a new topic, and December's focus is **using AI to dramatically improve go-to-market execution**.

- **hands-on** learning the new AI tools; we will have the experts to answer your deepest questions
- **curated in-person networking** so you find the right people in the room to move your career forward
