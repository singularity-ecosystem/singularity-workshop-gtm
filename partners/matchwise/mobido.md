# Mobido

Mobido is a smartphone app produced by ThirdPlaces/Matchwise that provides Privacy-first personal growth by using AI to reflect on everyday conversations.

## Mission

If we give AI a chance to listen to our everyday conversations and help us thoughtfully reflect, we stand to live much better lives.

However, most companies charge a high price — not in dollars — but in the loss of privacy.

Mobido is different. We put you in charge of your flow of ambient data such as conversations, and let you decide which AI is working for you. You can choose to keep everything on your smartphone with no Internet needed. Or you can run AI on your home computers under your complete control. Or you can use the cloud. The choice is yours!
