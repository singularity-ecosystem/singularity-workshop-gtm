# Singularity Workshop GTM

Go-to-market for Singularity Workshops: evenings where experienced professionals, students and new graduates, and career changers use an AI method on work they already have, meet someone who already works that way, and leave with a next step a partner can keep.

The first event is Thursday, December 10, 2026, 5:00–8:00 p.m. in San Jose, hosted with California Miramar University (CalMU). The acquisition target is 300 Luma registrations by the end of Tuesday, December 8.

## How the plan is built

Each layer is written so the next one can be checked against it. A later file does not replace an earlier one.

1. **Trends.** `vision/trends.md` collects the evidence: task exposure, displacement forecasts, and payroll and hiring results since late 2022. Early-career seats in the most exposed jobs have fallen. Older workers in those same jobs have gained seats. Employers are paying for AI skill and retraining the people they intend to keep.
2. **Markets.** `vision/target-markets.md` turns that split into the three groups the workshops are for, and only the groups CalMU or ThirdPlaces already reaches. A room event draws from San Diego, San Jose, and La Puente. Online students and professionals are the audience for what follows the room.
3. **Partner constraints.** `partners/` records what each organization is actually for. CalMU’s programs are a next step for students, veterans, and career changers. They are not the subject of the evening. ThirdPlaces and Mobido set the condition on the tools: the person chooses the AI and where it runs — on a phone with no internet, on a home computer they control, or in the cloud.
4. **Event goals.** `vision/event-goals.md` says what every evening has to produce (a method used on the attendee’s own work, an introduction matched to that work, a next step) and checks the December 10 draft against those outcomes and both missions.
5. **The evening, rewritten.** `events/calmu-dec-10/revised-event-description.md` keeps the short overview, the panel, and the networking from the original description, and adds the exercise, names the three groups, matches introductions by kind of work, and holds the showcase to the choice of where the AI runs.
6. **How the room gets filled.** `events/calmu-dec-10/gtm-brainstorming/` is three plans, each written to reach 300 registrations on its own. They share one calendar, one Luma page, and one “How did you hear about this?” question so a person asked twice is counted once. The plans are alternatives with a switch rule, not a stack that sums to 300.

## December 10 acquisition

The working window is three weeks from November 17, with campuses closed November 26–29 and San José State’s last day of instruction on December 7. New institutional asks after December 4 mostly become January conversations. At a usual weeknight show rate, 300 registrations put something like 120–180 people in the room, and finals week will push that down.

| Plan | Where the 300 come from | Fails when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Borrowed lists](events/calmu-dec-10/gtm-brainstorming/01-borrowed-lists.md) | Career centers and student orgs send a link they already know how to send | A center agrees and sends after December 7 |
| [Campus ground game](events/calmu-dec-10/gtm-brainstorming/02-campus-ground-game.md) | Class announcements, club meetings, and tabling while students are still on campus | Announcements start after Thanksgiving |
| [Workforce and veterans](events/calmu-dec-10/gtm-brainstorming/03-workforce-and-veterans.md) | Job centers, the library, adult education, and veterans offices refer people who want a skill | The library and both workforce boards all decline |

Borrowed lists is the highest-volume bet and fills the room with students. The ground game does not wait on a staffer to hit send. Workforce and veterans is the plan that fills the room with career changers and veterans, which the evening is built for and a student blast will under-deliver. Each plan states the morning on which a short count means switching the missing volume to another plan the same day.
